PURPOSE AND HOW TO READ THIS LOG
  This is the audit log for the Assessment Engine design. It records every
  inconsistency, gap, ambiguity, conflict and open decision found while
  writing ASSESSMENT.md, including issues that design introduced.

  After the first draft, a scope review was done at the owner's request.
  ASSESSMENT.md was cut back to the minimum the owner's requirements need,
  and its structure, depth and wording were aligned with the Planner,
  Orchestrator and Tutor specs. Everything deliberately left out is
  recorded in section 11 (S-01 to S-13), so nothing was dropped silently.

  No existing specification was edited. LEARNING_PLANNER.md,
  LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md, TUTOR.md, README.md, ASSESMENT.md and every
  AGENT.md file are unchanged. Each fix below is a recommendation for the
  project owner to approve as a coordinated change.

  Each entry has an ID (R-nnn), a severity (Critical / High / Medium / Low)
  and a status label:
    [Confirmed]  verified directly in the cited file text
    [Gap]        something no document defines (verified absence)
    [Inferred]   a risk reasoned from the design, not a contradiction in text
    [New]        introduced or exposed by the new ASSESSMENT.md
    [Open]       a product decision awaiting the owner
  Line numbers refer to the files as they are in commit c466657.

R-001  [Critical] [Confirmed gap]  Objective IDs are used everywhere but
       produced nowhere
  Location:
    LEARNING_PLANNER.md
      - sec 6 (lines ~55-59): course nodes have "expected outcomes"; a
        syllabus "lists topics" with "an identifier, title, priority, depth,
        and source references"; it also "defines how the learner will
        demonstrate the required outcomes". There is no objective entity.
      - It nevertheless refers to objectives: line 23 ("determine which
        objectives the learner demonstrated"), line 42 ("assessment evidence
        tied to learning objectives"), line 111 ("objective-level
        evidence").
    LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md
      - line 57 "Target objective IDs"; line 68 open_course returns "course
        objectives"; lines 85-87 report protocol "objective coverage",
        "evidence for each assessed objective"; lines 95-97, 133.
    TUTOR.md
      - line 51 "Objective ID and description, relevant syllabus topic
        identifiers, sequence, depth, expected outcomes". This implies an
        objective is a separate record linked to topics.
      - line 115 says the Planner provides "learning objectives". The Planner
        output contract does not.
      - lines 56, 72, 82.
    transcript_temp.txt
      - lines 506-514 recommend "brief learning objectives for each node".
        This was never carried into the Planner output contract.
  Issue: Three contracts treat objective_id as the key for teaching,
    assessment coverage, evidence and remediation. No contract creates
    objective records, assigns their IDs, links them to topics or outcomes,
    or validates them. The Planner text uses "objective" informally.
  Why it matters: Assessment coverage, per-objective evidence, Tutor
    remediation targets, recommend_review inputs and Learning Context
    updates cannot be implemented consistently without a shared key.
    Equating topic_id with objective_id silently would hide a modelling
    decision. One topic may need several assessable objectives, or one
    objective may span topics.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md defines a provisional "assessment
    target" with the minimum fields it needs (sec 5). It refuses to mint
    course objective IDs and allows only an explicitly labelled interim
    target_kind "topic" (R-036).
  Recommended resolution (PROPOSAL, not an existing decision):
    Add "assessable learning objectives" nested under syllabus topics in the
    Planner's syllabus output. Each objective has:
      - objective_id, unique within a curriculum version;
      - topic_id;
      - an observable statement;
      - a required depth / cognitive demand;
      - a priority, inherited from the topic unless overridden;
      - links to course expected outcomes;
      - a demonstration expectation;
      - source references.
    The Orchestrator's validation then also checks that:
      - every mandatory topic has at least one objective;
      - objective IDs are unique;
      - every expected outcome is covered by at least one objective.
  Coordinated changes needed:
    - LEARNING_PLANNER.md sec 6: syllabus/topic definition. Also
      build_curriculum and replan_curriculum outputs, recommend_review input
      (line 42), and AC2/AC3/AC5.
    - LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md: sec 5 inputs (start_assessment), sec 6
      outputs (open_course "course objectives"), sec 7 step 1 validation,
      and the reporting protocol.
    - TUTOR.md: sec 5 input line 51 (source of the objective record) and
      sec 9 line 115.
    - ASSESSMENT.md sec 5: replace "provisional" with the agreed contract.
    - Scaffold (currently empty): docs/AGENT-CONTRACTS.md,
      docs/DATABASE-SCHEMA.md, backend/app/contracts/models.py.

R-002  [High] [Confirmed]  The pre-planning diagnostic is not supported by
       the current Planner and Orchestrator flow
  Location:
    - LEARNING_PLANNER.md sec 5 line 40: build_curriculum inputs are "Goal
      preview" plus optional university references. There is no
      diagnostic/baseline input.
    - LEARNING_PLANNER.md sec 7 lines 63-64: the workflow goes straight from
      preview to drafting the DAG.
    - LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md:
      - sec 4: the action list has no diagnostic action;
      - line 52: build_curriculum inputs are a preview reference plus
        sources;
      - line 93 (workflow step 1): "Call the Planner's preview_goal and
        build_curriculum";
      - line 130 (AC1): "goal preview, then a validated full curriculum,
        then an optional course-entry familiarity check".
  Issue: The owner's new requirement puts an optional Assessment-led
    diagnostic between preview_goal and build_curriculum. No current
    contract carries a diagnostic report to the Planner or includes the step
    in the Orchestrator workflow. Because the diagnostic is optional, AC1's
    order still holds when it is skipped. With the diagnostic, the order
    becomes preview -> diagnostic -> curriculum, which AC1 does not
    describe.
  Why it matters: Without these changes the diagnostic cannot influence the
    DAG. Implementers may also route Assessment output straight to the
    Planner, breaking hub-and-spoke.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md defines the diagnostic as purpose
    "pre_planning_diagnostic" of prepare_assessment and grade_submission.
    Its output is a "diagnostic planning summary" for the Orchestrator to
    forward. No Assessment-to-Planner call exists.
  Recommended resolution:
    (a) Planner sec 5: add an optional build_curriculum input, "pre-planning
        diagnostic report (scoped planning summary)".
    (b) Planner sec 7 step 2: use the report when it is supplied, as
        evidence that informs depth and starting points. The Planner keeps
        academic judgement, and unknown areas stay unknown.
    (c) Orchestrator sec 7 step 1: offer the optional diagnostic after the
        preview. Forward only the planning summary, never keys or raw
        responses.
    (d) Orchestrator AC1: allow "preview -> optional diagnostic ->
        curriculum -> optional familiarity check".
    (e) Record explicitly that the diagnostic does not replace
        check_familiarity (Planner sec 8, line 80).
  Coordinated changes: LEARNING_PLANNER.md secs 5, 7 and 11;
    LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md secs 4, 5, 7 and 11; ASSESSMENT.md (already
    assumes this).

R-003  [High] [Confirmed]  start_assessment needs a Session ID, which does
       not exist before the curriculum; assessment purposes are not
       enumerated
  Location: LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md
    - line 57: start_assessment requires "Session ID and available
      assessment purpose";
    - line 68: sessions are created by open_course;
    - line 72: start_assessment returns "Saved attempt ID, questions, and
      instructions".
  Issue: Before build_curriculum there is no course and no session, so the
    current start_assessment cannot start a diagnostic. The set of
    "assessment purposes" is also never listed.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md defines four purposes. The diagnostic
    must reference a goal preview, not a curriculum version, course or
    session.
  Recommended resolution: choose one of these.
    Option A (recommended, fewer actions): start_assessment accepts EITHER
      a Session ID OR a goal-preview reference. For purpose
      pre_planning_diagnostic it requires the preview reference.
    Option B: add learner actions "start_diagnostic" and
      "decline_diagnostic".
    In either case, enumerate the purposes in Orchestrator sec 5:
      pre_planning_diagnostic, course_completion, targeted_check,
      post_remediation_verification.
  Coordinated changes: LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md secs 4-6 and possibly 11;
    ASSESSMENT.md sec 4 (attempt lifecycle note).

R-004  [High] [Confirmed gap]  The evidence taxonomy has no type for
       diagnostic evidence
  Location:
    - LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md line 103: three evidence uses, namely
      self-reports, Tutor observations and graded results.
    - LEARNING_PLANNER.md line 59: "states the reason and kind of evidence".
      The kinds are not enumerated.
  Issue: Diagnostic task answers are direct evidence, so they are not
    self-report. They are not course-completion grades either, and must
    never support progression. The taxonomy has no slot for them.
  Why it matters: If diagnostic evidence is stored as "graded results", it
    could unlock courses or carry forward. If it is stored as self-report,
    its direct-evidence value is lost.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md labels evidence_type as
    "diagnostic_task" or "graded_assessment". It forbids diagnostic evidence
    from progression and from carry-forward.
  Recommended resolution: Add a fourth type to Orchestrator sec 8:
    "diagnostic evidence informs initial planning and, if approved (R-033),
    familiarity recommendations; never progression". Enumerate evidence
    kinds in Planner sec 6: self_reported, diagnostic, assessed.
  Coordinated changes: LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md sec 8;
    LEARNING_PLANNER.md sec 6; DB schema (future).

R-008  [Medium] [Confirmed wording] [Inferred risk]  Learner context sent
       with the grading request can leak into grades
  Location:
    - LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md line 96: "Send the saved questions, criteria,
      and submission along with any learner context relevant to feedback";
    - line 105: "Learner context can inform question selection and
      feedback. Use the rubric saved with the attempt to determine the
      grade".
  Issue: The rule is correct, but the same call carries learner context.
    In a single LLM prompt, context such as self-reports, earlier struggles
    or "needs to pass to continue" can bias scoring.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md splits grading into two parts:
    - a context-free scoring step;
    - a feedback step that may use only a separate "feedback context"
      (language, accessibility, detail level).
    It forbids profile, self-report, prior grades, Tutor observations and
    progression status in the grading input.
  Recommended resolution: Change Orchestrator line 96 to send only a
    "feedback context" (language, accessibility, feedback preference) with
    grading, and state that scoring never receives learner context.
  Coordinated changes: LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md sec 7 step 4 and sec 8.

R-009  [Medium] [Confirmed gap]  The pedagogical model is not an Assessment
       input; the registry is undefined; the model is chosen per objective
  Location:
    - LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md line 13: "Choose a pedagogical model for the
      current objective". Line 96 lists Assessment inputs without the
      model.
    - TUTOR.md line 118: "The model registry and selection mechanism are
      outside this specification."
  Issue: The owner expects post-node assessment to be "informed by the
    pedagogical model", but the Orchestrator does not send it to
    Assessment. Models are chosen per objective, so one course assessment
    may span several models. No registry defines model identifiers.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md adds an OPTIONAL "pedagogical
    context", per target or uniform for the attempt. It restricts its use:
    - it may choose among equally aligned task formats and familiar
      presentation;
    - it may NOT change targets, depth, criteria, coverage or the pass rule.
    The assessment approach, which is fixed by purpose, is kept distinct
    from the pedagogical model.
  Recommended resolution:
    - Add "pedagogical context (optional, per objective)" to Orchestrator
      line 96.
    - Decide who owns the model registry, its identifiers and its teaching
      instructions: an Orchestrator configuration file is the simplest
      option.
    - Confirm that the "standards never change with the model" rule is
      acceptable.
  Coordinated changes: LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md secs 5, 7 and 9; a future
    registry doc.

R-010  [High] [Open]  The meaning of "pass" across several mandatory
       objectives is undefined
  Location:
    - LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md line 87: "Outcome ... apply the progression
      policy"; line 97: "A pass can make eligible successor courses
      available".
    - No document defines how a pass is computed.
  Issue: Many questions are unanswered:
    - Does pass require every mandatory objective?
    - Is a percentage threshold used?
    - How do partial or inconclusive results count?
    - Do recommended topics count?
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md sec 8 PROPOSES these MVP defaults:
    - Pass rule: every in-scope mandatory target must be "demonstrated".
      Any not_demonstrated or partial target gives not_passed. Otherwise,
      any inconclusive or not_assessed target gives inconclusive. Otherwise
      the outcome is passed. Recommended targets are non-blocking; optional
      targets are excluded unless assigned.
    - Fixed status mapping: an unscorable required criterion makes the
      target inconclusive; all required criteria met makes it
      demonstrated; no required criterion met or partially met makes it
      not_demonstrated; otherwise partial.
    - Missing answers: a blank in a course assessment is no_response; an
      unanswered diagnostic item leaves its area not_assessed.
    No percentage threshold is invented.
  Recommended resolution: The owner should approve or replace the rule.
    Keep one definition and reference it from the Orchestrator's
    progression policy (R-011).
  Coordinated changes: LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md sec 8; ASSESSMENT.md sec 8.

R-011  [Medium] [Inferred risk]  Assessment "outcome" and Orchestrator
       "progression policy" may be defined twice; ownership of the policy
       configuration is open
  Location:
    - LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md line 87 ("apply the progression policy");
    - line 106 ("Evaluate every required prerequisite branch before opening
      a dependent course").
  Issue: The Assessment outcome is per attempt. Progression combines
    outcomes, carried-forward evidence and prerequisite branches. If both
    documents define pass criteria, they can diverge. It is also unspecified
    who owns the assessment policy: pass rule, coverage, diagnostic budget
    and consistency check.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md receives the policy as an input from
    the Orchestrator and copies it into the attempt snapshot so that
    grading is reproducible. It never decides unlocks.
  Recommended resolution:
    - Single source: an "assessment policy" configuration owned by the
      project and supplied by the Orchestrator, with a version.
    - The Orchestrator's progression policy references attempt outcomes and
      per-objective evidence. It never recomputes grades.
    - Define "prerequisite branch cleared" in terms of objective evidence.

R-012  [Medium] [Gap]  Attempt lifecycle, attempt-ID timing and
       preparation retries are undefined
  Location:
    - LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md line 96: "Save the returned questions and
      criteria under an attempt ID" (when is the ID created?);
    - lines 72, 123 and 126: only "pending" is named.
  Issue: There is no attempt state machine (prepared / presented /
    submitted / grading_pending / graded / abandoned). It is unclear whether
    the attempt ID exists before prepare_assessment. Preparation is
    generative: a retried call can return different items, and the spec
    does not say which one is kept.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md requires the Orchestrator to assign
    the attempt ID before calling prepare. After the scope review it does
    NOT define attempt states, because the Orchestrator owns them (S-13).
    It states only its assumptions:
    - the report is saved before it is shown;
    - the submission is saved before grading;
    - pending carries no outcome;
    - the first saved prepared report is kept and later duplicates are
      discarded.
  Recommended resolution: Add an attempt lifecycle to Orchestrator secs 5
    and 7, for example prepared / presented / submitted / grading_pending /
    graded / abandoned. Also state that the ID is assigned before prepare.
    Define abandonment/expiry and whether an abandoned attempt counts.

R-013  [Medium] [Gap]  The "depth" vocabulary is undefined
  Location:
    - LEARNING_PLANNER.md lines 12, 57 and 79 ("depth", "at the needed
      depth");
    - TUTOR.md lines 12, 51 and 99.
  Issue: Depth is required everywhere but has no values or scale. Assessment
    cannot calibrate task demand deterministically, and the Orchestrator
    cannot validate the Planner's coverage rule (Planner line 79, AC3) in a
    checkable way.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md passes depth through as published,
    notes that the vocabulary is undefined, and aligns task demand mainly
    with the target statement and the demonstration statement. Without a
    scale, "required depth" cannot be checked mechanically.
  Recommended resolution: Define a small enumerated depth or
    cognitive-demand scale in the Planner. For example: recall / understand
    / apply / analyse. The CMU alignment page uses a related set of
    objective types adapted from revised Bloom's taxonomy (see R-040). Use
    it in the objective records (R-001).

R-014  [Medium] [Gap]  The syllabus's "how the learner will demonstrate the
       required outcomes" has no structure
  Location: LEARNING_PLANNER.md line 57.
  Issue: This is the most assessment-relevant output of the Planner, but it
    is free-form.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md uses it as the primary guide for task
    formats. Without structure, alignment cannot be checked.
  Recommended resolution: Represent it per objective as a demonstration
    expectation. For example: "solve and justify", "explain", "apply to a
    scenario", "derive".

R-015  [Medium] [Gap] [Open]  Carry-forward "when the rubric permits it"
       has no defined field; can targeted-check evidence count toward
       completion?
  Location: LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md line 104.
  Issue: No rubric field says whether evidence may carry forward. It is
    also unclear whether a targeted check, or a verification after
    remediation, can satisfy part of the course-completion requirement.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md PROPOSES that a "demonstrated" target
    may carry forward within the same course and curriculum version, and
    that diagnostic evidence never carries forward. The engine only marks
    eligibility; the Orchestrator combines evidence. Carrying evidence
    across curriculum versions, and "integrated" targets that must be shown
    together, were left out in the scope review and stay open.
  Recommended resolution: The owner approves the default and states in
    Orchestrator sec 8 how carried-forward and targeted-check evidence
    combine for completion.

R-016  [Medium] [Inferred risk]  Novelty exclusion ignores Tutor practice
       exercises
  Location:
    - TUTOR.md line 75: pending_exercise has an ID and prompt;
    - LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md line 104: "Use a fresh task to verify the
      same objective after review".
  Issue: A verification item that matches a practice exercise the learner
    just completed with a worked example (TUTOR.md line 78) is not a
    genuine check.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md accepts Tutor exercise prompts in the
    "excluded items" input (optional).
  Recommended resolution: The Orchestrator includes recent Tutor exercises
    for the same objectives in the exclusion list (PROPOSED MVP default).
  Coordinated changes: LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md sec 7 step 4.

R-017  [Medium] [Open]  Disclosure of solutions after grading is undefined
  Location:
    - LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md line 105: "Keep the active assessment and its
      answer key scoped to the attempt";
    - TUTOR.md line 56: remediation gets "Missed objective IDs and relevant
      learner-facing feedback. Formal assessment answer keys and private
      grading criteria are not required".
  Issue: It is unclear whether learners, and the Tutor through feedback,
    may see correct answers for a closed attempt.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md fixes only that nothing is disclosed
    while an attempt is open. It defers disclosure after grading to this
    entry ("Disclosure of correct answers follows R-017"). Proposed default
    (MD-13):
    - feedback may state the correct answer or idea for items in the
      closed attempt, because those items are excluded from future attempts
      for that learner;
    - rubric internals and grader rationale stay in the grading record.
  Recommended resolution: The owner decides. If no shared item bank is
    adopted (R-029), disclosure after closure is low-risk.

R-018  [Medium] [Open]  Skip threshold and skip path
  Location:
    - LEARNING_PLANNER.md line 83 ("pending product decision");
    - LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md line 106 ("proposed MVP default lets learners
      skip familiar instruction and proceed directly to an assessment for
      clearance").
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md PROPOSES that the skip path uses the
    same course_completion assessment: the same targets, coverage, criteria
    and pass rule, covering every mandatory target including skipped ones.
    "entry_path" is informational and never reaches scoring.
  Recommended resolution: The owner approves. Planner and Orchestrator then
    remove "open" wording and reference the assessment.

R-019  [Medium] [Gap]  Rules for conflicting evidence are not defined
  Location: LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md line 103: "Conflicting evidence can
    flag a specific objective for review while retaining both records".
  Issue: There are no rules for:
    - diagnostic evidence that conflicts with later course evidence;
    - a pass followed by a failed targeted check;
    - an inconclusive verification after a not_demonstrated result.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md deliberately gives the engine no
    prior evidence during grading, to keep grading context-free. Conflicts
    are therefore detected and resolved by the Orchestrator only.
  Recommended resolution: Orchestrator sec 8 should state that the latest
    graded course evidence decides progression. Earlier records are kept,
    and a disagreement flags the objective for a targeted check.

R-020  [High] [Inferred risk]  LLM rubric grading can be inconsistent
  Location: no spec covers grader reliability. IntelliCode itself
    acknowledges that LLM components vary and that guardrails do not fully
    remove this (PDF p. 7 / proc. p. 135, Limitations).
  Issue: The same response can be judged differently on a retry. Retries
    and duplicates (Orchestrator lines 123-124) can then yield different
    grades.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md:
    - grades closed formats deterministically;
    - ties rubric criteria to the saved level descriptors, with a rationale
      and response excerpt;
    - marks a criterion "unscorable" (target inconclusive) when the response
      cannot be placed on the levels;
    - relies on the Orchestrator recording the first accepted graded report
      for duplicates.
    The first draft also had a second, independent grading judgement for
    decisive criteria. It was removed in the scope review (S-01).
  Residual risk: Two gradings of the same open answer could differ, and only
    the first accepted one counts. Variability is accepted, not detected.
  Recommended resolution: Accept for the MVP. If grading disputes appear
    in testing, add the second judgement only for criteria that decide a
    mandatory target. Combine it with a dispute path (R-026).

R-021  [Medium] [Inferred risk]  Subject-general grading cannot assume
       automatically verifiable answers
  Issue: The platform targets any subject (physics, mathematics,
    humanities, research preparation). Code execution and test cases, as in
    IntelliCode (PDF pp. 3-4 / proc. pp. 131-132), do not generalise.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md does not assume code execution or
    automatic checking of open answers (S-08). Closed answers are graded
    deterministically and open ones against the rubric. Symbolic answers
    are deterministic only through listed equivalent forms. Code tasks are
    not among the supported formats.
  Recommended resolution: Confirm the supported formats. Decide whether an
    expression-equivalence checker or code sandbox is in MVP scope (both
    are proposed as out of scope).

R-022  [Medium] [Open]  Strength of evidence per target
  Issue: One item per objective is weak evidence, especially for selected
    response, where guessing is possible.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md PROPOSES:
    - every in-scope mandatory target is covered by at least one item;
    - targets about application, derivation or explanation need at least
      one open-response criterion.
    Each target reports an "evidence basis": the number of items and the
    response format. A labelled quality scale was left out (S-04).
  Recommended resolution: The owner approves the minimum, or sets a higher
    one against assessment length (R-025).

R-023  [Medium] [Inferred risk]  Novelty cannot be fully guaranteed
  Issue: Fingerprints and variation descriptors catch exact or near-exact
    repeats. Deep paraphrases remain possible. Objectives with a small task
    space, such as definitions, may have no genuinely different task.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md:
    - requires a different scenario or values AND a different expected
      answer where the format permits;
    - records a short note on how each item is novel;
    - flags novelty_limited items or returns novelty_unmet;
    - explicitly does not claim semantic novelty.
  Recommended resolution: Accept the stated limit. Optionally add a
    paraphrase-similarity check later.

R-024  [Medium] [Inferred risk]  Unassisted attempts cannot be monitored
  Issue: Formal attempts are assumed to be the learner's independent work.
    The Tutor must not help (TUTOR.md line 133), but the learner can use
    outside help. IntelliCode's hint-attenuated mastery (PDF p. 3 / proc.
    p. 131) does not apply to unproctored formal assessment.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md assumes the submission is independent
    work and does not monitor integrity (S-10). No spec says whether the
    Orchestrator may route teach_turn calls while an assessment attempt is
    open. TUTOR.md line 133 only forbids supplying answers to an active
    assessment. (Confirmed gap.)
  Recommended resolution:
    - Add to the Orchestrator: "no Tutor instruction on the assessed
      objectives while a formal attempt is open".
    - Record unproctored attempts as an MVP limitation in user-facing text.
    - Requiring explanation tasks, which the proposed coverage rule already
      does for higher-demand targets, partly mitigates the risk.

R-025  [Low] [Open]  Time limits, assessment length, retake limits and
       concurrent attempts
  Issue: No document defines:
    - a time limit or maximum length;
    - how many reassessments are allowed;
    - a cool-down between attempts;
    - whether a targeted check can run while a course_completion attempt is
      open.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md sets only a coverage minimum (MD-05)
    and the diagnostic budget (MD-08). It defines no maximum length for
    course assessments, no time limit and no retake rule. Concurrency is
    left to the Orchestrator.
  Recommended resolution: Owner decision. The simplest MVP: no time limit,
    one open attempt per course, unlimited reassessment after remediation.

R-026  [Medium] [Gap]  Wrong answer keys and no dispute path
  Issue: Generated keys can be wrong. The design re-solves derived keys and
    requires a source basis, but residual errors remain (see R-041). No
    document defines how a learner disputes a grade or how a wrong key is
    corrected.
  Recommended resolution: Add a simple "report a problem with this
    question" action to the Orchestrator. It marks the item disputed,
    excludes it, and offers a fresh check. Avoid re-grading other attempts
    in the MVP.
  Coordinated changes: LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md secs 4 and 10.

R-027  [Low] [Open]  No human educator oversight
  Location: IntelliCode recommends use "under the guidance of human
    educators" (PDF p. 7 / proc. p. 135). This project has no human
    reviewer role.
  Recommended resolution: Owner decision. For the MVP, state that the
    system is a learning aid and that its formal outcomes are not
    institutional credentials.

R-028  [Low] [Gap]  Accessibility and language accommodations
  Issue: No document covers these.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md accepts language and accessibility
    needs as selection and feedback context. They never affect criteria.
  Recommended resolution: Add accessibility preferences to the Learning
    Context fields.

R-029  [Low] [Open]  No shared item bank; items are generated per attempt
  Issue: Generating every item costs time and money and carries a risk of
    key errors. A curated shared bank would improve reliability, but it
    needs curation and raises the cross-learner answer-exposure question
    (R-017). transcript_temp.txt line 290 suggested a "tagged diagnostic
    question bank" as part of the rejected research-grade proposal.
  Recommended resolution: The MVP stays with per-attempt generation, as in
    ASSESSMENT.md. Revisit later.

R-030  [Medium] [Open]  No grade scale or numeric score
  Location: LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md uses "grade" (lines 97, 105) without a
    scale.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md PROPOSES no percentage or numeric
    score in the MVP: categorical outcome plus per-target statuses.
  Recommended resolution: Owner confirms, or defines a scale with its use.

R-051  [Medium] [New]  ASSESSMENT.md needs fields that current
       Orchestrator contracts do not carry
  Issue: To stay consistent without editing other specs, ASSESSMENT.md
    defines inputs and outputs that the Orchestrator does not yet send or
    store:
    - assessment policy (pass rule, coverage minimum, diagnostic budget,
      version);
    - excluded items (IDs and text, optionally including Tutor exercises);
    - entry path (skip vs study);
    - pedagogical context (R-009);
    - selection context vs feedback context (R-008);
    - goal-preview reference for the diagnostic (R-003);
    - prepared report split into a learner presentation and a grading
      snapshot;
    - graded report fields: criterion judgements, evidence_type, evidence
      basis, recommended_next_action, diagnostic planning summary;
    - assessment issue codes;
    - Assessment's own action names, prepare_assessment and
      grade_submission. The Orchestrator names only the learner actions
      start_assessment and submit_assessment.
  Recommended resolution: Extend Orchestrator secs 5-7 (inputs it
    assembles, records it saves) and the reporting-protocol table at lines
    83-87. Record them in docs/AGENT-CONTRACTS.md and
    docs/DATABASE-SCHEMA.md when those are written.

R-054  [Low] [Confirmed gap] [New]  The Orchestrator does not handle
       failures when preparing an assessment
  Location: LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md sec 10 (lines ~117-126). It covers
    invalid curricula, malformed reports, Tutor interruption and grading
    timeouts, but not assessment preparation failing, timing out or
    returning coverage_unmet / novelty_unmet / target_ungrounded.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md returns typed, retryable or
    non-retryable issues. The Orchestrator has no rule for what the learner
    sees, or whether a non-retryable issue goes to the Planner for review.
  Recommended resolution: Add a row to Orchestrator sec 10:
    - retryable preparation issue: retry with the same attempt ID and show
      "assessment temporarily unavailable";
    - ungrounded target or unmet coverage: keep progression pending and
      request Planner review of that target.

3. PRE-PLANNING DIAGNOSTIC WORKFLOW CONFLICTS

  (R-002, R-003 and R-004 above are the main conflicts.)

R-031  [Medium] [Inferred risk]  Choosing diagnostic areas versus the
       Planner's academic ownership
  Location: LEARNING_PLANNER.md line 78: "The Planner chooses the courses,
    links, topic order, and depth".
  Issue: To probe baseline knowledge before any DAG exists, Assessment must
    choose areas to probe. That resembles choosing prerequisites, which is
    the Planner's academic role.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md:
    - derives probe areas only from the preview milestones and from
      prerequisites stated in approved sources;
    - labels the areas as attempt-scoped and not curriculum IDs;
    - makes every planning implication advisory.
    Area priority is PROPOSED: milestones first, then source-stated
    prerequisites. Stated background is accepted only as unverified
    self-report. Using it to pick task depth was left out in the scope
    review.
  Recommended resolution: Optionally let preview_goal return "candidate
    foundation areas" so the Planner seeds the diagnostic. This would be an
    optional Planner output field. Otherwise, accept the advisory model.

R-032  [Medium] [Open]  Diagnostic budget
  Issue: No agreed number of questions, rounds or duration.
  Impact on Assessment: After the scope review, ASSESSMENT.md PROPOSES a
    SINGLE round of at most 10 prompts and tasks in total, self-report
    prompts included. The optional follow-up round was removed (S-02). This
    follows the "5-10" bound in the transcript's review of decision 4
    (transcript_temp.txt lines 448-450). The bound was never confirmed as a
    product decision. No duration is proposed.
  Recommended resolution: Owner approves or changes the values.

R-033  [Medium] [Open]  Keeping the diagnostic separate from course-entry
       familiarity, and possible reuse
  Location: LEARNING_PLANNER.md line 41: check_familiarity accepts optional
    "Prior familiarity or assessment evidence".
  Issue: The diagnostic and the familiarity check must stay separate
    workflows, as the owner requires. Existing Planner text would, however,
    let diagnostic evidence be passed into check_familiarity as prior
    evidence. That could shorten the familiarity conversation, but it might
    also be read as merging the two.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md keeps them separate. The familiarity
    conversation still happens after publication.
  Recommended resolution: Owner decides whether the Orchestrator may pass
    diagnostic evidence (labelled diagnostic, R-004) into check_familiarity.
    Recommended: yes, as optional input only. It must never skip the
    conversation automatically.

R-034  [Low] [Confirmed]  "Diagnostic answers" is a leftover term in the
       Planner
  Location: LEARNING_PLANNER.md line 89: "Personal plans, diagnostic
    answers, and progress are private to the learner".
  Issue: The Planner defines no diagnostic. This is probably left over from
    the earlier diagnostic design. It now matches the new diagnostic's
    privacy needs.
  Recommended resolution: Keep it, but define the term by reference to the
    pre-planning diagnostic once R-002 is approved.

R-035  [Medium] [Gap]  Diagnostic edge paths
  Issue: Several cases are undefined:
    (a) the learner refines the goal after the diagnostic; the preview
        reference changes;
    (b) a replan or goal change; should the diagnostic run again?
    (c) whether the learner sees the diagnostic results;
    (d) how long diagnostic evidence is retained;
    (e) the learner declines halfway through.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md handles (e): the attempt is graded
    with diagnostic_partial and unanswered areas are not_assessed. For (a)
    and (b), the report keeps its goal-preview reference so the
    Orchestrator can judge relevance. (c) and (d) are not covered.
  Recommended resolution:
    - (a) The Orchestrator forwards a diagnostic report only if its preview
      reference matches, or the owner accepts reuse.
    - (b) Offer it again on a goal change only.
    - (c) Show the learner a short, plain summary (recommended for
      transparency).
    - (d) Follow the privacy rules for learner data.


R-053  [Medium] [Confirmed gap] [New]  The Orchestrator report protocol
       assumes every graded attempt feeds progression
  Location: LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md
    - line 87: an Assessment graded report means "Record the evidence and
      apply the progression policy";
    - line 97: "Commit a valid grade, objective evidence, and the resulting
      progress change together".
  Issue: A diagnostic attempt has no course, no curriculum version and no
    pass/fail. Its result must NOT go through the progression policy. The
    protocol has no branch for attempts that do not feed progression.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md labels diagnostic outcomes
    "diagnostic_complete" / "diagnostic_partial" and the evidence type
    "diagnostic_task". The Orchestrator still needs a rule that routes
    these attempts to storage and to the Planner only.
  Recommended resolution: Add to the "Assessment graded" row in Orchestrator
    sec 6: "for a pre-planning diagnostic, record the evidence and forward
    the planning summary; apply no progression change".
  Coordinated changes: LEARNING_ORCHESTRATOR.md secs 6-7 (together with
    R-002 to R-004).

R-055  [Low] [Inferred risk] [New]  The learner may be asked about their
       background three times
  Location:
    - LEARNING_PLANNER.md line 39: preview_goal takes optional
      "Clarifications about interests, intended direction";
    - ASSESSMENT.md: diagnostic self-report prompts;
    - LEARNING_PLANNER.md sec 7 step 5: course-entry familiarity
      conversation.
  Issue: The same learner may answer background-style questions in the
    preview, again in the diagnostic and again at each course entry. This
    adds friction, which runs against the owner's "efficient and
    learner-friendly" requirement.
  Impact on Assessment: ASSESSMENT.md accepts background the learner has
    already stated as input, so diagnostic prompts can skip what is already
    known. It does not require this.
  Recommended resolution: The Orchestrator passes preview clarifications
    into the diagnostic, and the diagnostic asks only for background not
    already given. Keep familiarity questions syllabus-specific (Planner
    rule unchanged).

R-036  [Medium] [New] [Proposal]  Interim compatibility using

  Issue: The Orchestrator cannot check that every expected outcome is
    assessable, and Assessment cannot report coverage of outcomes.
  Recommended resolution: Link each objective to one or more outcome IDs
    (part of R-001). Give outcomes IDs.

7. PROPOSED MVP DEFAULTS NEEDING OWNER APPROVAL
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  All of these appear in ASSESSMENT.md marked "proposed MVP default".

  MD-01 (R-010)  Pass rule.
                 Every in-scope mandatory target must be demonstrated.
                 not_demonstrated or partial gives not_passed; otherwise
                 inconclusive or not_assessed gives inconclusive; otherwise
                 passed.
  MD-02 (R-010)  Target status mapping, in order.
                 Unscorable makes the target inconclusive; all met makes it
                 demonstrated; none met or partially met makes it
                 not_demonstrated; otherwise partial.
  MD-03 (R-010)  Target scope.
                 course_completion covers every mandatory target.
                 Recommended targets are included only when the Orchestrator
                 asks, and never block. Out-of-scope topics are never
                 assessed.
  MD-04 (R-010)  Missing answers.
                 A blank course item is no_response; an unanswered
                 diagnostic item leaves its area not_assessed.
  MD-05 (R-022)  Coverage.
                 At least one item per in-scope mandatory target.
                 Application, derivation and explanation targets need at
                 least one open-response criterion.
  MD-06 (R-022)  Evidence basis.
                 Each target reports the number of items and the response
                 format. Replaces the earlier quality labels (S-04).
  MD-07 (R-020)  WITHDRAWN in the scope review.
                 Second independent grading judgement (S-01).
  MD-08 (R-032)  Diagnostic budget.
                 A single round, at most 10 prompts and tasks in total.
  MD-09 (R-031)  Diagnostic area priority.
                 Milestones first, then source-stated prerequisites.
  MD-10 (R-015)  Carry-forward.
                 Within the same course and curriculum version only. Never
                 from the diagnostic.
  MD-11 (R-018)  Skip path.
                 Same assessment, same standard, every mandatory target.
  MD-12 (R-016)  Exclusion list.
                 Includes earlier assessment and diagnostic items and,
                 optionally, recent Tutor exercises.
  MD-13 (R-017)  Disclosure after grading.
                 Correct answers may be shown for closed items; rubric
                 internals are not.
  MD-14 (R-030)  Score.
                 No numeric score or percentage; categorical outcome only.
  MD-15 (R-036)  Interim topics.
                 target_kind "topic", only while R-001 is unresolved.
  MD-16 (R-012)  WITHDRAWN from ASSESSMENT.md in the scope review.
                 Attempt states belong to the Orchestrator (S-13). R-012
                 keeps the suggested states as a recommendation for the
                 Orchestrator.
  MD-17 (R-023)  Novelty rule.
                 No fingerprint match, and a different scenario or values
                 AND a different expected answer where the format permits.
